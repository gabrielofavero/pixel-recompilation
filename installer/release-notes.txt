Arkedo Series - 03 PIXEL! 1.0.0

Windows x64 fan port using FNA. Bring your own supported Xbox 360 LIVE package;
the original game and its assets are not included in these downloads.

Install
-------
Download PIXEL-1.0.0.exe and run it. Select your Xbox 360 LIVE package, choose
installation/save folders and shortcuts, and let setup prepare the game locally.
No separate .NET installation or internet connection is required during setup.
Only the supported package is accepted (SHA-256):
64b5bb62e8cf16c44c04c1b9d2ac5f1c780db1cd28e031cd4315788ab3af4104

Features
--------
- Controller, keyboard, and mouse menu support, with automatic button prompts.
- Ultrawide gameplay. Menus and button puzzles retain their 16:9 layout.
- Help and Options, including windowed, borderless, and fullscreen display modes.
- Local saves, preserved across reinstall and uninstall.

Keyboard quick reference
------------------------
Move/navigate: WASD or arrows. Jump/confirm: Space or Enter. Run: Shift.
Interact/cancel: E or Backspace. Scan: hold Tab. Special action: Q.
Pause/resume: Esc or P. Back/exit in menus: Esc or Backspace.
Mouse: click menu options; left click in gameplay also interacts.

Saves and support
-----------------
Setup defaults saves to the Windows Saved Games folder under
"Arkedo Series - 03 PIXEL!" and allows another folder. Player.sav.bak retains
the previous save; malformed saves are preserved as Player.sav.corrupt-*.
Startup logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Pixel Recompilation\Logs\run.log
Report issues at https://github.com/gabrielofavero/pixel-recompilation/issues
Include the release version, Windows/GPU details, and the relevant log.
Do not upload your game package, extracted assets, or prepared game files.
